Manage Apple container on this Mac — and on the rest of your Macs.

Flotilla wraps the container CLI. It runs Linux containers as one micro-VM each on Apple Silicon, and Flotilla drives it here and across your fleet over a mutually-authenticated TLS link.

Local first Containers, images and volumes on this Mac, with logs and a real terminal.
Then the fleet Each remote Mac runs the same app in host mode. Discovered by Bonjour, or added by hostname.
